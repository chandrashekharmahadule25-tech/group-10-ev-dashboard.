Feedback triage

Approved knowledge base (demo)

Use these as response structures only. Replace general guidance with the exact approved vehicle, warranty and policy documents before customer use.

How much range will I get?Real-world range varies with speed, traffic, temperature, payload, terrain, driving style, battery condition and use of climate control. Check the model-specific specification and independent test conditions; do not treat a claimed range as a guarantee.
Where can I charge?Check current charger location, connector compatibility, operating status, access rules and payment method in the relevant charging operator’s app before travelling. The prototype does not provide live charger availability.
Am I eligible for a subsidy?Eligibility depends on current scheme rules, vehicle category, date, registration and available allocation. Confirm against the latest official notification and authorized dealer; this dashboard cannot determine eligibility.
How do I compare ownership cost?Compare purchase price, financing, electricity, fuel avoided, maintenance, insurance, charging equipment, battery/warranty terms and resale assumptions over the same distance and ownership period.
